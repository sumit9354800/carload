Founded in 2004, Auto Mark India Pvt. Ltd. has grown to become a trusted name in the financial services sector. We are a premier Direct Selling Agent (DSA) connecting customers with India’s top banks and NBFCs.

Over the past 20 years, we have helped thousands of families and businesses secure the financing they need. Our approach is simple: listen to your needs, provide honest advice, and find the best options from our network of 14+ lending partners.
